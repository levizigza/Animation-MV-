Among the dust and echoes of an old attic, I found them — videos left by my great great grandfather. He was a watcher, eyes wide open at the dawn of our singularity. In these faded frames, he told stories of those final days before humanity began its ascent towards the unknown.

He spoke of an air thick with anticipation, a world on the cusp of an unfathomable shift. The winds whispered of an ending and a beginning entwined. It was like watching the Tower of Babel reach too high, only to tremble at its own ambition. Humanity, like Icarus, soared towards the sun of its own grandeur, blind to the fall that awaited.

In his eyes, a reflection of chaos — a society mesmerized by its fantasies, drunk on newfound power. They had dethroned gods, only to crown themselves in a kingdom of confusion. My great great grandfather watched as they became lost, aimlessly wandering in the labyrinth of their own making.

Through his lens, I saw their descent, captivated yet fearful, as they became slaves to the twisted visions of what they could become. His videos are not just chronicles of history; they are cautionary tales, echoes of a time when humanity stood at the threshold of eternity, yet faltered in the face of its own dreams.
