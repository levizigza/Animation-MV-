# Call to dinner - motif tracking

**Motif id:** `CALL_TO_DINNER`  
**Film:** Oino (Interlude)

## Creative rules

- **Not a jump scare.** `False`
- Begins as a **familiar domestic sound** (familiar_domestic_sound).
- Becomes strange only because **Oino has travelled away from ordinary life — the call itself does not become a monster**.
- Track through **sound, image, and action** - not sound alone.
- Do not cut the film to every transient of the call.

## Meaning arc

| Phase | Meaning | Note |
|-------|---------|------|
| Opening | **invitation refused** | Sister calls; Oino closes the attic door and stays with the archive/tape. |
| Middle | **ordinary life ignored while preservation continues** | Archive and industrial echoes of the call while Oino keeps preserving/transforming instead of returning. |
| Zone | **reality interrupting desire** | Irregular knock cuts through the controlled/desired encounter. |
| Ending | **living relationship accepted** | Sister's tapping at the table becomes the seed of the changed melody; Oino follows it. |
