## Characters and lineage

- The protagonist is **Oino**, a young woman.
- Oino is the modern inheritor of the Oino/Oeno name and mythic lineage.
- The mythology is an **artistic adaptation**. It does not claim that one genealogy or one cultural tradition is universally correct.
- Mythic family line used by this project:

  **Dionysus → Staphylus → Rhoeo → Anius → Oino**, followed by Oino's later descendants.

- Oino's inherited gift is **transformation toward nourishment**, expressed symbolically through music, memory, food, care, and change.
- **Dionysus** is a major presence in the larger project. This interlude should connect to him **without** requiring a literal god character to explain the plot.

## Story objects (do not invert)

- The **original monologue remains unchanged**.
- The monologue is spoken by a young woman who narrates the **great-great-grandfather's archive**.
- The **sister's call to dinner** is the emotional and mythic trigger of the story.
- The **father's unfinished melody** is the film's main musical object.
- The great-great-grandfather is a **watcher**: perceptive, loving, fallible, and implicated in the failure he describes.
- The **industrial revolutions** show changing tools and landscapes while recurring human needs remain recognizable.
- Every technological era must contain a **benefit and a cost**.
- The **table** is the film's version of the mythic destination: ordinary, intimate, and potentially sacred.
- The reunion with the father must **genuinely comfort Oino** before the shadow appears.
- **Oino's shadow** appears when she rewinds, edits, and controls the encounter until it cannot surprise her.
- Oino's **holy-fool action** is allowing the father to leave while she still wants him to stay.
- The ending changes through **behaviour**: she returns to her sister, accepts anger, and follows the sister's altered note.
- The final image resolves into the exact title card **One Of Gods Fools** and **December 10, 2026**.

## Creative rules — Oino and Dionysus

1. Do **not** give Oino generic magical powers. Let the myth live through choices, recurring images, and transformations of sound and material.
2. Do **not** treat Dionysus as only wine, partying, or chaos. Use the full tension between life-force and loss of self.
3. Do **not** make Dionysus the villain of technology. Technology can relieve exhaustion, enable speech, preserve memory, and support connection.
4. Do **not** make Oino's choice an argument that suffering must continue. Her choice concerns agency, separateness, and the freedom to change.
5. Do **not** make the father a monster beneath a digital face. The temptation must remain beautiful.
6. Do **not** turn the sister into a moral prop. She is a living person with her own rhythm, anger, and creativity.
7. Do **not** literalize every reference to Babel, Icarus, Orpheus, Inanna, Midas, or Dionysus.
8. Do **not** explain the Zone. Allow it to remain memory, simulation, spiritual place, or something beyond those categories.
9. Do **not** synchronize every edit to every beat. Let the music breathe and let irregularity interrupt mechanical repetition.
10. Whenever an image looks impressive, check whether the audience still understands **who is waiting, who is leaving, who is listening, and who is choosing**.
