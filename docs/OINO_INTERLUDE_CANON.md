# Oino Interlude — project canon

Canonical copy for the repo. Authoritative project file also lives at
[`projects/oino-interlude/CANON.md`](../projects/oino-interlude/CANON.md).

**Title card (final image):** *One Of Gods Fools* · December 10, 2026

## Characters and lineage

- Protagonist: **Oino**, a young woman; modern inheritor of the Oino/Oeno name and mythic lineage.
- Mythology is an artistic adaptation — not a claim of one universally correct genealogy.
- Line: **Dionysus → Staphylus → Rhoeo → Anius → Oino** (+ later descendants).
- Gift: transformation toward nourishment (music, memory, food, care, change).
- Dionysus is present in the larger project; this interlude connects without requiring a literal god as plot explainer.

## Story objects

- Original monologue unchanged; young woman narrates the great-great-grandfather's archive.
- Sister's call to dinner = emotional/mythic trigger.
- Father's unfinished melody = main musical object.
- Great-great-grandfather = perceptive, loving, fallible watcher, implicated in the failure he describes.
- Industrial eras: changing tools/landscapes; recurring human needs; each era has benefit and cost.
- Table = mythic destination (ordinary, intimate, potentially sacred).
- Father reunion must genuinely comfort Oino before the shadow.
- Shadow = rewind/edit/control until the encounter cannot surprise her.
- Holy-fool action = letting father leave while still wanting him to stay.
- Ending via behaviour: return to sister, accept anger, follow sister's altered note.
- Final image: exact title card **One Of Gods Fools** and **December 10, 2026**.

## Creative rules

No generic magic powers for Oino. Full Dionysian tension (life-force / loss of self), not only wine/party/chaos. Technology is not Dionysus-as-villain. Oino's choice is agency/separateness/freedom to change — not “suffering must continue.” Father's temptation stays beautiful. Sister is a living person, not a moral prop. Do not literalize every mythic name. Do not explain the Zone. Do not sync every edit to every beat. Always keep clear: who waits, leaves, listens, chooses.
